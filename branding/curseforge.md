### The Peggle Institute has opened a branch in Azeroth... again!

**Peggle Forever** is a free, unofficial fan edition of PopCap's Peggle add-on, made for **WoW Forever**. It keeps the classic peg-popping game running and adds heroes, talent trees, more levels and a few surprises.

Aim, shoot, and clear every orange peg before you run out of balls. Hit a green peg to unleash your hero's ability.

## Features

**8 heroes, one for each Classic race.** Each hero has their own signature ability, voice lines and ability sounds taken from the game itself:

| Hero | Race | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| Bolvar Fordragon | Human | **Heroic Strike**: your next shot smashes straight through up to 8 pegs |
| Magni Bronzebeard | Dwarf | **Earth Shield**: a stone shield saves your next lost ball |
| Tyrande Whisperwind | Night Elf | **Starfall**: a shower of stars that favors orange pegs |
| Gelbin Mekkatorque | Gnome | **Thorium Grenade**: a bomb with a fuse, plus cluster bombs |
| Thrall | Orc | **Chain Lightning**: lightning that jumps from peg to peg and sometimes forks |
| Sylvanas Windrunner | Undead | **Black Arrow**: a guide line for your next shots, plus shadow arrows |
| Cairne Bloodhoof | Tauren | **War Stomp**: a shockwave that ripples across the board |
| Vol'jin | Troll | **Hex**: curses pegs; hit one and they all detonate |

You start as the hero for your character's race and can switch at any time.

**Talent trees.** Every hero has their own 15-talent tree, and you earn points by beating levels with that hero. Deeper talents change how the ability works, and there are capstones at the bottom. You can't fill the whole tree, so every build involves choices. A shared **Legacy tree** brings back the original Peggle talents, and you fill it by mastering levels across all your heroes.

**23 levels.** You get the 13 original add-on levels, plus 10 levels re-created from the desktop game *Peggle: World of Warcraft Edition*, moving pegs included.

**Play with friends:**
- **Duels:** a 1-on-1 ten-ball match against another player.
- **Battles:** challenge players to beat your score on a level, whenever they like.
- **Peggle Loot** (`/peggleloot`): your party or raid takes one shot each, and the high score wins the item.

In duels and Battles you pick the hero everyone plays as, and talents are switched off, so it's a fair fight.

**And more:**
- A Stats page with lifetime and per-hero records.
- Three appearances: Original, Horde red or Alliance blue. It starts with your faction's.
- A minimap button, options to open and close the window automatically (on flights, on death, in combat), and Escape closes the window.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/peggle` or `/peg` | Open or close the game |
| `/peggle help` | List all commands |
| `/peggle stats` | Your stats and records |
| `/peggle resetwindow` | Move the window back to the middle of the screen |
| `/peggleloot [item]` | Start a Peggle Loot round for your party or raid |

## Good to know

- **Peggle Forever is made for WoW Forever.** It is built and tested only on that client and may not work in other versions of World of Warcraft.
- Peggle Forever uses its own multiplayer channel, so it can't duel players who use the original Peggle add-on. Both players need Peggle Forever.
- Don't enable it together with the original Peggle add-on, because they share window and command names. If both are on, Peggle Forever switches itself off and tells you why.
